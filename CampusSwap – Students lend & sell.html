How it works

1. List it

Add photos-free details, price and how long it is available.

2. Connect

Interested students WhatsApp you and meet on campus to check the item.

3. Pay by UPI

The buyer taps Pay and the amount opens in their UPI app. Money goes straight to you.

Stay safe: meet inside the campus, inspect the item before paying, and keep the UPI payment screenshot. CampusSwap does not hold money; deposits are returned by the lender when the item comes back.
